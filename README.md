Leetify - Elevate Your Coding Interview Preparation

# Features

- Plan Your LeetCode Journey: Easily create and manage your LeetCode study plan.

- Collaborative Learning: Share your LeetCode plans with others and benefit from shared knowledge.
- Access Community Plans: Explore and utilize LeetCode plans created by fellow developers.
- Visual Progress Tracking: An intuitive dashboard to monitor your plan's progress.
- Stay Inspired: Find motivation through plan completeness rankings.
